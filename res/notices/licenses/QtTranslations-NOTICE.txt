Qt community translations

The installed qtbase_*.qm files provide translations of Qt's own standard
controls and dialogs. This delivery selects ar, de, es, fr, it, ja, ko, pl,
pt_BR, ru, tr, uk, zh_CN and zh_TW when available in the build environment.
Qt does not provide a Hindi catalog in the tested distribution; Vulkana's own
Hindi controls are translated, while untranslated Qt-owned dialogs fall back
to English. These are separate, unmodified data files, not Vulkana's
independently authored localization/*.ts catalogs.

Local validation used qt6-qttranslations-6.11.2-1.fc44.noarch, whose distributor
declares GPL-3.0-only WITH Qt-GPL-exception-1.0. Copies of GPL-3.0 and the Qt
exception are included. The Qt Company Ltd. and Qt translation contributors
retain their copyrights. The distro license declaration covers the aggregate
module, including tools; it is not a claim that the BSD build scripts or every
individual Qt file use identical terms.

Upstream source for the tested translation data:
https://github.com/qt/qttranslations/tree/v6.11.2/translations
https://github.com/qt/qttranslations/blob/v6.11.2/licenseRule.json

Upstream's license rules list LGPL-3.0-only among the module-file options and
separate the GPL-with-exception tool terms from BSD build scripts. Preserve the
upstream and distributor declarations when preparing distribution notices;
do not infer a different file license just from the Qt library's license.

Binary release preparation must record the exact source/version of its chosen
Qt translation catalogs and include that matching qttranslations source in
the release's Qt source/notices bundle. A different build environment may
supply different catalog versions and must update its distribution inventory.

Translations are contributed by the Qt community without guarantees. Vulkana
does not change the original translation or license texts.
