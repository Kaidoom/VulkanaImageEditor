Vulkana exports the complete image encoder and prompt decoder to ONNX opset 17
using the upstream model and checkpoint. The trained weights are not fine-tuned.
The exported graphs are derived artifacts, not the original .pt checkpoint.
Vulkana's preprocessing, boundary recovery and selection integration are separate
application code. Python, PyTorch and the development environment are not shipped.
